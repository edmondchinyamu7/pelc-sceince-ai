Engineering Learning Centre

⚙️

Simple Machines

Learn about levers, pulleys, wheels and axles, inclined planes, wedges and screws. These machines help us change the size or direction of a force.

Mechanical advantage = Load ÷ Effort

🔌

Electrical Engineering

Explore voltage, current, resistance and circuits. Learn how components work together to transfer electrical energy.

V = I × R

🤖

Robotics Fundamentals

A robot can use sensors to collect information, a controller to make decisions, and actuators such as motors to perform actions.

  • Sensors: detect changes
  • Controllers: process information
  • Actuators: create movement
🧩

Engineering Design

Engineers identify a problem, research possible solutions, design a prototype, test it and improve the design.

Problem → Design → Build → Test → Improve

⚙️

Gears and Motion

Gears transfer rotational motion. In a simple pair of gears, a smaller driving gear generally makes a larger driven gear rotate more slowly.

Speed ratio = Driver teeth ÷ Driven teeth

🌱

Engineering in Malawi

Explore engineering ideas for local needs, including irrigation, solar-powered systems, water management, bridges and agricultural equipment.

Engineering Safety

Learn with diagrams, simulations and low-risk classroom models. Do not experiment with household mains electricity, exposed live wiring, high-voltage equipment or heavy machinery without qualified supervision. Real engineering projects need appropriate safety checks.

Agricultural Science Lab

Discover how crops grow, explore soil science, calculate planting spaces and understand irrigation through interactive agricultural tools. Learn practical concepts relevant to farmers and students in Malawi.

Explore Agricultural Science

Learn the science behind food production and sustainable farming.

🌱

Crop Production

Learn about seed selection, germination, planting, weeding, nutrient management, harvesting and post-harvest storage.

🌍

Soil Science

Explore soil texture, structure, drainage, organic matter and nutrients. Different crops grow best under different soil conditions.

💧

Irrigation

Understand how water reaches crops through watering cans, drip irrigation, sprinklers and other systems. Good scheduling helps reduce unnecessary water use.

🌦️

Climate and Weather

Study rainfall, temperature, sunlight and seasonal changes. These factors affect planting dates, crop growth and harvesting.

🐛

Pests and Plant Health

Learn to recognise signs of pests and plant diseases, monitor crops and explore integrated pest management and prevention methods.

🚜

Sustainable Agriculture

Explore crop rotation, composting, erosion control, agroforestry and soil conservation to support long-term agricultural productivity.

Agriculture in Malawi

🌽 Maize Production

Study germination, soil fertility, rainfall, planting density, weed management and post-harvest storage. Suitable practices depend on the variety and local conditions.

🥜 Legumes

Explore crops such as groundnuts, beans and soybeans. Legumes can contribute to farming systems through food production and, for suitable varieties, biological nitrogen fixation.

🌳 Soil and Water Conservation

Learn about mulching, contour farming, cover crops and other locally appropriate methods for reducing soil erosion and protecting moisture.

Responsible Farming and Lab Safety

These tools are educational estimates. Follow product labels and qualified agricultural guidance when handling fertilisers, pesticides or other farm chemicals. Do not taste unknown seeds or plants. Check local recommendations before making real farm decisions.

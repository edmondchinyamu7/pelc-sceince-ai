Explore Environmental Science

🌳 Ecosystems

An ecosystem includes living organisms and the non-living environment interacting in a particular area.

💨 Air Pollution

Smoke, dust and harmful gases can affect human health, wildlife and the atmosphere.

💧 Water Pollution

Wastewater, chemicals and litter can contaminate rivers, lakes and groundwater.

🌡️ Climate Change

Human activities, especially greenhouse-gas emissions, are driving long-term global warming.

♻️ Waste Management

Reduce waste first, reuse items where possible, and recycle suitable materials.

🦋 Biodiversity

Biodiversity is the variety of life. Protecting habitats helps species and ecosystems survive.

1. Ecosystems and Food Chains

Organisms depend on one another and on resources such as sunlight, water, air and nutrients. A change in one part of an ecosystem can affect other parts.

Example food chain

Sun → Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake
  • Producers: make their own food, usually through photosynthesis.
  • Consumers: obtain energy by eating other organisms.
  • Decomposers: break down dead material and recycle nutrients.

2. Climate Change

Greenhouse gases

Carbon dioxide, methane and other greenhouse gases trap heat in Earth's climate system.

Possible impacts

Impacts include changing rainfall patterns, heat extremes, rising sea levels and risks to ecosystems.

Taking action

Saving energy, protecting forests, reducing waste and choosing lower-emission transport can help.

Remember: weather is the condition of the atmosphere over short periods; climate is the pattern of weather over many years.

4. Environmental Challenges in Malawi

🌲 Deforestation

Forest loss can reduce habitats and increase soil erosion. Tree planting works best alongside protecting existing forests.

🌧️ Soil Erosion

Heavy rain, loss of vegetation and unsuitable land use can carry fertile soil away. Ground cover and contour farming can help.

🏞️ Water Protection

Keeping rubbish and hazardous waste out of rivers and lakes helps protect water quality and aquatic life.

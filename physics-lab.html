🚗

Motion and Speed

Motion is a change in position over time. Speed describes distance travelled per unit of time.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Worked example

100 m travelled in 20 s gives a speed of 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s.

🧲

Forces and Newton's Laws

A force is a push or pull. A net force can change an object's velocity.

F = m × a
Worked example

A 2 kg object accelerating at 3 m/s² has a net force of 6 N.

⚡

Work and Energy

Kinetic energy is energy of motion. Work is done when a force transfers energy through displacement.

KE = ½ × m × v²
Worked example

A 2 kg object moving at 3 m/s has kinetic energy of 9 J.

🔌

Electricity

Current is the flow of charge, voltage is potential difference, and resistance opposes current.

V = I × R
Worked example

A current of 2 A through a 4 Ω resistor gives a voltage of 8 V.

🌊

Waves and Sound

Waves transfer energy. Frequency is the number of complete cycles per second.

Wave speed = Frequency × Wavelength
💡

Light and Optics

Optics studies light, including reflection, refraction, mirrors, lenses and image formation.

Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection
🌡️

Heat and Temperature

Heat is energy transferred due to a temperature difference. Temperature measures how hot or cold something is.

Q = m × c × ΔT
🌍

Gravity

Gravity is an attractive interaction between masses. Near Earth's surface, g is approximately 9.81 m/s².

Weight = Mass × g

📐 Formula Library

Common formulas for Physics exercises.

Speed

v = d ÷ t

Distance divided by time.

Acceleration

a = (v − u) ÷ t

Change in velocity divided by time.

Force

F = m × a

Mass multiplied by acceleration.

Weight

W = m × g

Mass multiplied by gravitational field strength.

Kinetic Energy

KE = ½mv²

Half the mass multiplied by speed squared.

Ohm's Law

V = I × R

Current multiplied by resistance.

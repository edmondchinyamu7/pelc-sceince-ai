Explore Physics Topics

Physics studies matter, energy, motion and the interactions that explain natural phenomena.

01. Motion

Motion describes how an object's position changes with time. Speed measures distance travelled per unit time; velocity also includes direction.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Example: A runner covers 100 m in 20 s.

Speed = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s.

02. Forces

A force is a push or pull that can change an object's motion or shape. The SI unit is the newton (N).

F = m × a

Example: A 3 kg object accelerates at 2 m/s².

Force = 3 × 2 = 6 N.

03. Energy

Energy is the capacity to do work. Kinetic energy is associated with motion, while gravitational potential energy depends on height.

KE = ½mv²

Energy is measured in joules (J).

04. Electricity

Electric circuits allow charge to flow. Voltage is potential difference, current is charge flow per second, and resistance opposes current.

V = I × R

Voltage is measured in volts (V).

05. Waves & Light

Waves transfer energy. Frequency measures oscillations per second, and wavelength is the distance between matching points on a wave.

v = f × λ

Frequency is measured in hertz (Hz).

06. Heat & Temperature

Temperature relates to how hot or cold something is. Heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference.

Q = m × c × ΔT

This applies when specific heat capacity is constant.

07. Pressure

Pressure is force acting per unit area. It is important in solids, liquids and gases.

P = F ÷ A

The SI unit is the pascal (Pa).

08. Density

Density tells us how much mass occupies a given volume.

ρ = m ÷ V

The SI unit is kg/m³.

Physics Formula Library

Review the formula, the symbols and their standard SI units.

Speed v = d / t

d = distance (m), t = time (s), v = speed (m/s).

Acceleration a = (v − u) / t

v = final velocity, u = initial velocity, t = time.

Newton's Second Law F = ma

F = force (N), m = mass (kg), a = acceleration (m/s²).

Weight near Earth's surface W = mg

W = weight (N), m = mass (kg), g ≈ 9.81 m/s².

Kinetic Energy KE = ½mv²

m = mass (kg), v = speed (m/s), KE = energy (J).

Gravitational Potential Energy GPE = mgh

m = mass (kg), g = gravitational field strength, h = height (m).

Electrical Ohm's Law V = IR

V = voltage (V), I = current (A), R = resistance (Ω).

Wave Speed v = fλ

v = wave speed (m/s), f = frequency (Hz), λ = wavelength (m).

Density ρ = m / V

ρ = density (kg/m³), m = mass (kg), V = volume (m³).

Pressure P = F / A

P = pressure (Pa), F = force (N), A = area (m²).

Always check that quantities use compatible units before substituting values into a formula.

LEARN BY SOLVING

Worked Examples

Example 1: Calculate speed

A cyclist travels 150 metres in 30 seconds. Find the average speed.

  • Formula: speed = distance ÷ time
  • Substitute: speed = 150 ÷ 30
  • Answer: 5 m/s

Example 2: Calculate force

A 4 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s². Find the net force.

  • Formula: F = ma
  • Substitute: F = 4 × 3
  • Answer: 12 N

Example 3: Calculate kinetic energy

A 2 kg ball moves at 5 m/s. Find its kinetic energy.

  • Formula: KE = ½mv²
  • Substitute: KE = ½ × 2 × 5²
  • Answer: 25 J

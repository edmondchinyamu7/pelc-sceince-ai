Cell Biology

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms. Animal and plant cells share structures, but plant cells also have a cell wall and usually a large central vacuole.

Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ system

Remember: The nucleus contains most of the cell's genetic material. Mitochondria are important in aerobic respiration.

🧬

Genetics and DNA

DNA stores genetic information. Genes are sections of DNA, and inherited versions of genes are called alleles.

DNA → Genes → Inherited traits

In a simple dominant/recessive model, an organism's genotype can influence its observable phenotype.

🫀

Human Biology

Human organ systems work together. The circulatory system transports materials, the respiratory system exchanges gases, and the digestive system absorbs nutrients.

Example

The heart pumps blood through blood vessels, helping deliver oxygen and nutrients to tissues.

🌱

Plant Biology

Plants use photosynthesis to convert light energy into chemical energy. Roots absorb water and minerals, while leaves are major sites of photosynthesis.

Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

Light energy and chlorophyll are involved in photosynthesis.

🌍

Ecology and Ecosystems

Ecology studies how organisms interact with one another and their environment. Ecosystems include living organisms and non-living factors.

Producer → Primary consumer → Secondary consumer

Food chains show one pathway of energy transfer; food webs show interconnected pathways.

🦎

Evolution

Evolution is change in inherited characteristics of populations over generations. Natural selection can make inherited traits more common when they improve reproductive success in a particular environment.

Key idea

Individuals vary, some variation is inherited, and reproductive success can differ.

🦠

Microbiology

Microbiology studies microscopic organisms and agents, including bacteria, many fungi, protozoa and viruses. Viruses need host cells to reproduce.

Good hygiene, safe food handling and appropriate vaccination can help prevent some infectious diseases.
🥗

Nutrition and Health

The body uses nutrients for energy, growth, repair and normal functioning. A varied diet can supply carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water.

Food → Digestion → Absorption → Use by cells

📐 Key Biology Concepts

Magnification

Magnification = Image size ÷ Actual size

Image and actual sizes must use the same units.

Germination Percentage

Germination % = Germinated ÷ Planted × 100

Use counts from the same seed sample.

Population Density

Density = Number of organisms ÷ Area

For this calculator, the area is measured in square metres.

Photosynthesis

Atoms and Elements

An atom contains protons and neutrons in its nucleus, with electrons occupying regions around it. An element is defined by its number of protons.

Atomic number = Number of protons
Example

Carbon has atomic number 6, so a carbon atom has 6 protons.

🧩

Periodic Table

The periodic table arranges elements by atomic number. Elements in the same group often have similar chemical properties.

Mass number = Protons + Neutrons
🔗

Chemical Bonding

Ionic bonding involves electron transfer and attraction between ions. Covalent bonding involves shared electron pairs.

Example

Sodium chloride contains sodium ions and chloride ions held together by electrostatic attraction.

🧪

Chemical Reactions

Chemical reactions rearrange atoms to form new substances. Equations must conserve each type of atom.

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
Example

Four hydrogen atoms and two oxygen atoms are present on each side of this balanced equation.

⚖️

The Mole

The mole is a unit for amount of substance. One mole contains approximately 6.022 × 10²³ specified particles.

Moles = Mass ÷ Molar mass
🧴

Acids, Bases and pH

In aqueous solutions, pH describes acidity. A pH below 7 is acidic, 7 is neutral, and above 7 is alkaline at about 25°C.

pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]
💧

Solutions and Concentration

A solution is a mixture of solute and solvent. Molar concentration is the amount of solute per litre of solution.

Concentration = Moles ÷ Volume (L)
🧬

Organic Chemistry

Organic chemistry studies carbon compounds, including hydrocarbons and compounds containing functional groups.

Alkanes: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂

📐 Chemistry Formula Library

Amount of Substance

n = m ÷ M

n = moles, m = mass, M = molar mass.

Molar Concentration

c = n ÷ V

c = mol/L, n = moles, V = solution volume in litres.

Mass Concentration

Mass concentration = mass ÷ volume

Common unit: g/L.

Density

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

Use consistent units, such as g/cm³ or kg/m³.

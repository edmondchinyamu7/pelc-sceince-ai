1. Algebra

Algebra uses letters to represent numbers. To solve a simple linear equation, perform the same operation on both sides to isolate the unknown.

Worked example: Solve 3x + 5 = 20

Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 15.

Divide both sides by 3: x = 5.

Check: 3(5) + 5 = 20.

3. Statistics

Statistics helps us understand data. The mean is the sum divided by the number of values. The median is the middle value after sorting. The mode is the most frequent value, and the range is the maximum minus minimum.

Worked example

Data: 2, 4, 4, 6, 9

Mean = 25 ÷ 5 = 5.

Median = 4; mode = 4; range = 9 − 2 = 7.

4. Percentages

A percentage expresses a value as a fraction of 100. To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100.

Worked example

Find 15% of 200.

15 ÷ 100 × 200 = 30.

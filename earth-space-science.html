Explore the Universe

🌍 Earth's Structure

Earth has a crust, mantle, outer core and inner core. These layers differ in composition and physical state.

🌙 The Solar System

Eight planets orbit the Sun. Gravity keeps planets, moons and many other objects in orbit.

🌦️ Weather & Climate

Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions. Climate describes patterns over long periods.

🪨 Rocks & Minerals

Igneous rocks form from cooled magma or lava, sedimentary rocks from accumulated material, and metamorphic rocks from changed existing rocks.

🌗 Day & Night

Earth's rotation causes the regular cycle of day and night. One rotation takes approximately 24 hours.

⭐ Stars & Galaxies

Stars produce energy through nuclear fusion. Our Solar System belongs to the Milky Way galaxy.

1. Understanding Earth

The water cycle

Energy from the Sun drives evaporation. Water vapour cools and condenses into clouds, then returns to Earth's surface through precipitation. Water can collect in rivers, lakes and oceans or enter the ground.

Earth's movements

  • Rotation: Earth spins on its axis, producing day and night.
  • Revolution: Earth travels around the Sun in about 365.25 days.
  • Seasons: Earth's tilted axis, combined with its orbit, causes seasonal changes.

2. The Solar System

☿ Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun.

♀ Venus

Has a very dense atmosphere and an extremely hot surface.

🌍 Earth

Our home planet, with abundant surface liquid water.

🔴 Mars

A rocky planet often called the Red Planet.

🟠 Jupiter

The largest planet in the Solar System.

🪐 Saturn

A gas giant famous for its prominent ring system.

🔵 Uranus

An ice giant with an unusually tilted axis of rotation.

🌊 Neptune

A distant ice giant with very strong winds.

☀️ The Sun

The star at the centre of our Solar System and its main energy source.
